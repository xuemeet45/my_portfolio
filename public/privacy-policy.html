Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 15, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how SumitAuto collects, uses, and shares information when you use our TikTok automation service.

1. Information Collection

We access only the minimum data required for functionality:

  • TikTok Account Data: Username, profile picture, and account ID
  • Google Drive Content: Only videos you explicitly select for upload
  • Metadata: Video titles, descriptions, and creation dates

2. Data Usage

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Processing automated video uploads to your TikTok account
  • Temporary caching during transfer operations
  • Service improvement analytics (aggregated and anonymized)

We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary:

  • OAuth tokens are stored until you revoke access
  • Video content is deleted immediately after processing
  • Logs are purged after 30 days

4. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of stored data
  • Deletion: Ask us to erase your information
  • Revocation: Disconnect via TikTok Settings
